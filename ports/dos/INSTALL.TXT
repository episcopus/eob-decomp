EYE OF THE BEHOLDER: FASTER BUILD FOR THE TANDY 1000 EX

This is the same game, rebuilt from its decompiled source with the drawing, menu and intro code tuned for an 8088 with
Tandy video, and with new sound for the Tandy's sound chip. The game plays the same way: same screens, same saved
games. Three files change and two are new:

  EOB.EXE     the game program
  INTRO.EXE   the logos and the introduction
  TGA.OVL     the Tandy graphics driver
  SN.DAT      the game's sounds for Tandy sound (new)
  SNSOUND.DAT the introduction's music and sounds for Tandy sound (new)

They were built from version 1.9 of the game (the copy this project works from). Use them with that version.

INSTALL

1. Make a copy of the three original files on your EX, in the game directory:

     COPY EOB.EXE EOB.ORG
     COPY INTRO.EXE INTRO.ORG
     COPY TGA.OVL TGA.ORG

2. Copy EOB.EXE, INTRO.EXE, TGA.OVL, SN.DAT and SNSOUND.DAT from this folder into the game directory, over the
   originals (keep them together; a mix will not work well). With Tandy sound the game will not start without
   SN.DAT and SNSOUND.DAT.

3. Run the game the usual way (START1, Tandy graphics, Tandy sound). Saved games and everything else are shared with
   the original.

To go back: COPY EOB.ORG EOB.EXE, COPY INTRO.ORG INTRO.EXE and COPY TGA.ORG TGA.OVL (SN.DAT and SNSOUND.DAT can stay;
the original does not read them).

The game needs about 3 KB more memory than the original (the text tables and the new routines in TGA.OVL). SHA256.TXT
lists the checksums of the five files. SN.DAT and SNSOUND.DAT take no memory of their own: they are loaded where the
AdLib sound driver sits, which is unused with Tandy sound.

WHAT CHANGES

With Tandy sound, every sound effect is new: made for the Tandy's three tones and noise channel and chosen by ear, one
for each thing the game does (footsteps and attacks for each monster, each spell, doors, levers, items). The original
Tandy version played only about half of the game's sounds; this one plays all of them, quieter when a monster is far
away, as the AdLib version does, and the music of the party creation screens, which it did not have. The introduction's
music and sounds are new too, taken from the AdLib's: all three of its pieces of music (the original Tandy version had
two), with the sound effects playing over the music as they do on the AdLib. Because the introduction's pictures now
come faster, its music runs longer than the scenes it was written for: the title music starts just before the logo
music ends. The PC speaker and AdLib sound are as they were.

The logo and title dissolves take about 1.6 seconds each instead of about 15 (the title 3 instead of 30). A spell hit's
sparkles (magic missile, fireball, lightning and the rest) run at the game's own pace, about 2 seconds instead of 4 to 5.
Cone of cold takes about 11 seconds instead of 36. Moving the highlight in the camp menu takes about 0.06 seconds of
work per key press instead of 0.3, and each menu page draws about 2 to 2.5 times faster. A step or a turn in the dungeon
is about 1.6 times faster than the original. Loading a level does about 4 seconds less work and makes 86 DOS read calls
instead of about 6,000, which should matter more on the real machine than these figures show. These figures are from a
model of an 8088 at 7.16 MHz, which does not include disk or DOS time; the real machine is the final judge.

One thing is left out on purpose: the camp menu no longer checks the far heap before each page (a debugging check that
printed "Far heap corrupt!" if memory had been damaged). It took a quarter of a second at every key press.
