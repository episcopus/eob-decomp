The VGA palette is loaded twice in the life of a level: once at start-up from `EOBPAL.COL`, and again when a level with its own `.PAL` is entered. Below, for each wall set, which of the 256 entries differ from the start-up palette and which indices the set's two colour tables ask for: the wall colours sit at D0h and above, and the colours the interface names (82h, 84h, 85h, 87h, B1h, B4h, B8h, 11h, 14h, 17h) are the same in every file.
