## Summary

The unit is understandable from its source alone. The region record and the globals are backed by the initial table and by 1DE7's accesses, and the names from the earlier review (region, not image) hold. Two comments are wrong: the header's "cut into" claim (the records overlap, record 0 is the full screen) and the condition under which 1DE7:02FE calls the selector. Function names stay placeholders (`f1A29_xxxx`), which is fine: `Purpose unknown` is still true for the front ends' roles, and `extra` in 1A29:0107 and 1A29:0148 has no caller to show what it is. `make match FILE=src/E1A29.C` is 7 of 7 and `make readability UNIT=e1a29` is 0 findings, so the matcher and the gate have nothing to add here.
