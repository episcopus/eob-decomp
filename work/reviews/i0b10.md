  Fix the sentence to name the two callers, e.g. "Unpack (0AED:00CB, format 0) and ReadFromMemory
  (0B5D:1236, a copy from an in-memory file) call Unpack0."
- [ ] should src/I0B10.ASM:2-3: the six-byte gap is explained by a cause that covers only one of

  Say that, e.g. "the EOB unit starts at 1BA8:0005 (its data byte) and this one at 0B10:0000, so

## Summary

The unit is understandable from its source alone and its names are backed by evidence: `Unpack0`
is the format-0 raw copy (I0AED.C:68, E1B82.C:68), `NormFar` is the normalizer whose behaviour the
body shows (AX = offset & 0Fh, DX = segment + offset >> 4), and `NormalizeFarPointer` is supported
by its callers in the EOB twin. One must-fix: the header's sentence about Unpack0's callers names
"format-0 unpackers", but ReadFromMemory is not an unpacker; fix the sentence. The rest are should
or note items (parameter layout, the twin's placeholder name for the normalizer, the six-byte
explanation, and labels). `make readability UNIT=i0b10` reports 0 findings; the readability rules
for functions (R3, R4) are for C files, so the reader's guidance comes from this file's comments.
