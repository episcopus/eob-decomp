## Summary

The unit is understandable from its source, and its structure is right: the start-up order, the argument decoding, the overlay table, the segment table and the shut-down order all match the owners' evidence. The bytes match (5 of 5 functions, 1610 bytes) and the readability check is clean. Ten must-fix findings remain, all in comments or in a few names, not in code. Three are flag or meaning claims that are the wrong way round or unsupported (gMouseDisabled, gTandyOn, gSpeakerMode); two are wrong facts about the sound and video (the gSoundCard 0 driver is AdLib, not the PC speaker, with the labels crossed; "restores video mode 7" is really the argv[5] mode); two are wrong text (gTextAttr is a text attribute, the Tandy driver does not pause); and three are names or types the evidence does not give (EnvBlock/gEnvPtr, the gMemTopSeg video-top reading, the OverlayRequest arguments). Placeholders are left on purpose where no evidence names them yet: g8102, g80EA, g3056, g3092, gFn80FA, gFn8056, f07C8_061D, f1D8F_0010, f1D8F_0038, f1A47_0005. No matcher or spec question; nothing escalated.
