## Summary

The unit reads well from its source alone: Main, StartupGame and ShutdownGame say what the start-up and shut-down do, in game terms, and the names of the callees and globals agree with the other units. All five functions match and `make readability` passes, so the work left is about meaning and wording. The must-fix items are concentrated in three places: the graphics-choice numbering (the "modes" in the palette comments are switch case labels, and choice 0 gets no palette), the palette buffer comment (only two bytes are written), and a handful of wrong line citations (:153, :218, E1A72 :70-74, the `ds:8894` description). The placeholder names that remain are: `f1DDD_002D`, `f1D8F_0010`, `f1D8F_0038`, `f1A47_0005`, `f1A47_0169` (callees, with evidence in their units, to be named by their owners); `f07C8_061D` (marked Purpose unknown, correctly: it is the default exit hook); `g8894` and `Unk8894` (Purpose unknown, correctly: nothing in src/ writes ds:8894); `g80EA` (readers known, comment should say so); `g3056` and `g3092` (placeholders with complete comments). Nothing is escalated: no matcher or spec question was found.
