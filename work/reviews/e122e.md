## Summary

The unit reads as the script interpreter it is: the driver (RunMemberScript), its 26 handlers, and the
expression evaluator. The struct sizes add up by hand (SlotRegion 52 bytes, CharRec 243, Item14 14, DDRec 18,
Obj28 28), and the field offsets agree with the file's own comments; I did not re-check every offset
against the other units. Most names
are backed by a cited address or a caller. The must-fix items are the comments that describe a
condition, a direction or an argument wrongly: the m0D+m0F pick, the inset rectangles, the roll in
DamagePartyMember, the on-screen test in MoveCarriedItemOp, the 2067 sheet address, and two cited
claims that the owning units do not make.

Still placeholders, and fine as placeholders: `fOvl5_1A82` and `fOvl4_2039` (use the shared names,
above), `gTable18BF` ("Purpose unknown", no evidence yet), `Obj0150` and `Obj1505` (shared with
src/E08E6.C), `ObjectCountIsOdd` (the name says "odd" and the function returns bit 0; the name
fits the test, not a meaning), and `WriteObjectFieldOp` and the other handler names (named for what
each switch does, with the opcode in the comment).

One question is not this unit's to settle. The meaning of `gPartyCell` (the sound volume or the
party's map cell) is open in src/E08E6.C:97-103 and asks this review and the E0ACD review to settle
it. From this file, the evidence goes both ways (see the must-fix at line 196), and
SetSoundVolume's name depends on the answer. This is a cross-unit naming decision: it should go to
the human (or to the E08E6 and E0ACD owners) rather than be settled by one review. No matcher, spec or pin
question.
