## Summary

The unit is understandable from its source alone for the mechanics: every function has a comment
with its address, names that the other units already use (Obj0E48, Obj17FD, Obj15B4, gObjs,
gB894, gTable1A3B), and quirks marked. The main problems are wrong claims in comments, not wrong
code: the table at ds:1A3B is called character records, `m10` is called a sound field (it is the
target cell), several callers and counts are wrong (0E48's callers, 0EC3's m14 roll, Obj1505's
field count, BuildObjectIdList's array size, 189D's callers), and the control-flow descriptions of
0765 (hits every member), 10F3 (m1D, negates) and 1410 (inverted count test) do not match the code.
The prototypes of PlaySoundAtCell and DirectionFromPoints name a cell parameter `volume`. No name
is a bare placeholder without reason: the Obj#### names follow the addresses, and the ones with
purposes are marked `Purpose unknown:`. Nothing is escalated: no matcher, spec or pin question.
