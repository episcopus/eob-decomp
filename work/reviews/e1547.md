## Summary

The unit is readable and its layout is well described: the screen positions, the per-member tables and the match notes are clear, and most of the function names fit what the code draws. Eleven must-fix items block the pass. Three are wrong facts about the code's own callers and modes (who calls DrawMemberHp, DrawMemberPortrait, DrawMemberLevel and DrawMemberSlot; what mode 1 draws; a mouse test read the wrong way). Two are wrong claims about data: the stat labels at +11 to +1D (the armour-class byte is +1D, and the stat names are one slot off), and the food meter named as a level (m23 is hunger, by EatFood and DecayMemberState). Two more are inverted or wrong formulas (the highlight polarity and the wound-state test). One is a "Purpose unknown" the unit's own status lines and E1414.C already explain (m01 bits 1 and 2). The rest are shoulds and notes: placeholder names still in use (frame and lit, gF43C, gFB7A), and cross-unit type disagreements (mE8, m23, mE7, m1E, exp) that the owners of those units need to settle. Nothing is escalated: no matcher, spec or pin question. The stat label order is read from EOB.EXE with grep; the ds:1195 pointer order is not yet checked and the fix should confirm it.
