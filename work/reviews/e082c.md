### Summary

one wrong cite (the gTicks handler range, :66). Round 3's fixes hold. Placeholders that stay because the
evidence is missing: LoadScreenImage's `extra` and DrawShadowText's `colour2` (both Purpose unknown). DrawBevelBox,
DrawShadowInt, QuitGame and DrawTextAndMemoryReport have no callers in src/, so their names rest on their bodies;
that is enough. The four DrawRegion wrappers are address-named (a should above). Not checked this round: the
three-pixel width cite at src/EOV5.C:413 (NumberWidthLong's header), the EditTextField cite to src/E14EF.C:146
(IsPointInRect's test), and the gFileNameBuf and gOverlayBuf names in files other than E082C.C. Nothing
escalated: no matcher, spec or pin question.
