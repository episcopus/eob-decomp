## Summary

The unit reads well from its source alone, and the names that carry claims (the key codes, the clamp and wrap, the block swap, the field's clip rectangles and the mode table) are backed by cites I checked and found correct. Two things block the pass: the mode table and CPS cites point at the wrong lines (E07C8.C:31-34 and E082C.C:274), and FormatName is described as an art file name when its one caller builds a level file name (EOV3.C:568). The should items are about names and headers that do not say what the parameters mean (colour, attr, cursorAttr, the second colour in DrawShadowText, the struct fields that are still saved0 to saved3 while E1DE7 has attr1 to row), and about callers and cites that are missing. Placeholders that remain are backed by "Purpose unknown" or by the same placeholder the neighbouring units use. Nothing is escalated: no matcher, spec or pin question.
