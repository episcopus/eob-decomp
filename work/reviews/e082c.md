## Summary

The unit is understandable from its source for the main entry 082C:000B and the drawing helpers, and the bytes are proved by the matcher. But seven claims in it do not hold: the g8102 mode table (3 is VGA, not CGA), the "save and restore" of the field (ClipRect does not save), the wrong cross-reference name (PointInRect), the unevidenced key codes (KeyDelete also is '~'; KeyLeft and KeyRight have no scancode cite), a function named a rotation that swaps two blocks, a number helper described as centring that right-aligns, and a clamp that wraps in its only use. Placeholder names that stay are fine: DrawRegion000F and the `extra` parameter are placeholders with partial evidence, and g8102 has enough evidence for a rename. No escalation: no matcher, spec or pin question is raised here.
