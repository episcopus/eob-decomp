## Summary

Both names hold up on the bytes. `_LoadOverlayImage` is a 0FA00h far block from MemAlloc (flag 20h), an INT 21h/4B03 load, and a 0 or 0FFFFh return; `_RunOverlayRequest` is called from `main` (07C8:0199) at 07C8:0254 to 0260, which builds the request at DS:8121 and compares AX with -1. The per-function comments match the code I re-read, apart from the points above.

The unit header is not yet right. It gives the request layout wrongly (the second word is the name's segment, and the entry arguments are at +4 and +6), and it says 1C00:0055 allocates a record, which it does not. Both are must-fix. The 1C00:0055 comment says the flags are "driver present" and "clears g814A", but leaves out what the flags mean in the mouse handler (src/E1A72.ASM:43, 236), and the leak mark is not observable because the caller returns from main. These are should items.

Placeholders that remain, all for lack of evidence: the overlay-loader words at DGROUP:818Ah to 8194h, the entry arguments 27h and 1A16h, and the pad byte at 1C00:0001. Review fails until the two must-fix items in the unit header are corrected.
