## Summary

The unit is understandable from its source alone. Its five functions (Main, StartupGame, ShutdownGame, ExitHook, AllocationFailure) have names that match their roles, and the claims that matter check out against other units: the INT 3Fh and hook installs, the 64 KB memory reserve (I0923.ASM header and the 0923:0133 notes), AllocationFailure's role as the allocation-failure hook (E07C8.C:491), the restore-mode index 7 (E1DDD.C), the graphics choices (1 Tandy, 3 VGA, the START1 menu order in the whitepaper, section 2.6), and the sound choices (1 AdLib, 2 PC speaker, 3 Tandy).

Three claims are wrong and are must-fix: the file header's list of what Main reads, the name and claim for the word at 0923:0006 (gMovedSeg, not gMemTopSeg), and the description of gSoundBuf (the 120-byte sound map, not a driver buffer). The rest are should items: g28C4 is gMemTopSeg and its purpose is known; g232C and g0E6C_00B8 need what they are; ShutdownGame's comment misses its callers (if the packet is right); and the palette numbers and memtop's type need naming.
