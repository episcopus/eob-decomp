## Summary

The unit is understandable from its source for the overall plan: the title, logo and scene order, the driver and sound calls, the image loader and the copy helpers are described, and the file names of the scenes check out against the strings in the program. What it cannot yet do is be trusted in the details. Many scene comments describe a sequence, a count or a caller the code does not have (the hand phases, the GrowFrame and DrawGrid callers, the tunnel and avalanche steps, the king's direction and random phase, the blit mode 3, the tile table, the mage page), and several values quoted in comments are wrong (the Westwood and SSI logo delays). Two names are not right: `gUnknown1EE2` disagrees with `gFn1EE2` in src/I0892.C for the same address, and the second parameter of `f19EC_002A` is a slot, not a mode. The placeholders the file uses on purpose (the tile, star, king and zoom tables, `gDriverState`) are marked `Purpose unknown:` and that is accepted; the placeholders of the 0998 routines belong to their owner. Round 1 has 17 must-fix items: the review fails. Nothing is escalated: no matcher, spec or pin question was found.
