## Summary

The source is understandable from its code alone. ClipRect does what the code shows: it caps the width and height, raises a negative origin to 0 with its partner origin and the size, and returns early when an x origin reaches SCREEN_COLUMNS. The names are evidenced, and gFar8052 and the extras are honest placeholders. The one must-fix is the header's caller survey: "The five call sites" and "The two callers" present five of about 30 callers as the whole set (packet, and the named callers in EOV2.C, E1DE7.C and EOV6.C). The shoulds are the eight-int sentence, the sign of the size shift, and the caller evidence for the columns reading, which the header says does not exist. The origin-pair lead from E1DE7.C:245 is worth testing in a later round, not a finding now.
