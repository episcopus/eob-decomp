## Findings

Each finding is a checkbox. `must-fix` blocks `make review-pass` until a decompiler ticks it off
(with the commit that fixes it); `should` and `note` do not block.

- [ ] must-fix src/E19F3.C:11-13: the header says "The x1 test runs first, before the negative-x1 shift; the x2
  test runs after it". The code does the reverse for x1. The negative-x1 shift (lines 44-48) comes first, then the
  x1 test (line 49, `if (x1 >= 40) return;`), then the x2 test (line 50), then the negative-x2 shift (lines 51-55).
  Evidence: 19F3:0030..0039 is `or di,di` / `jge`, the shift and `xor di,di`; 19F3:003B `cmp di,28h` is the x1 test,
  and 19F3:0040 `cmp si,28h` the x2 test. Say the x1 test sees x1 after its shift (so a negative x1 never makes it
  return), the x2 test follows and sees x2 after the negative-x1 shift has moved it, and the negative-x2 shift comes
  after both tests. Also say that the shift adds -x1 to x2 (`x2 -= x1`), not x1. The conclusion that "a clipped x1
  can push x2 to 40 and make the function return" stays true.

- [ ] should src/E19F3.C:40-66: the literals 40 (four uses: the width cap, the two early returns and, in the
  header, the 40 columns) and 200 (the height cap) are limits of the screen (40 columns of 8 pixels is the 320-pixel
  screen, 200 is its lines). READABILITY rule 4 asks for named constants for limits. For example
  `#define SCREEN_COLUMNS 40` and `#define SCREEN_LINES 200`, with the 320x200 screen evidenced at src/E1A72.ASM:176
  (the DGROUP screen bounds and the 320x200 defaults). src/ has no `#define` yet, so this sets the first one; it is
  a choice for the decompiler.

- [ ] note src/E19F3.C:21-27 (the header's example list, not a finding): 082C:006D pushes extra2=2, extra1=0,
  height=9, width=[bp+e]+2, y2=0xbf (191), x2=0, y1=[bp+c], x1=[bp+a]-1 (082C:0049..006C). So the bottom 9 lines
  are copied (191 + 9 = 200). That is evidence for the [H] reading of y as lines and of y2 as a destination origin,

## Summary

The unit is understandable from its source alone. `ClipRect` names what the bytes do (clip the top and left origins
to 0 with their partners and the size, cap the size, return early for an origin at or past 40 columns), and the
parameter names x1 to height are backed by the caller values and by the region table in src/E1A29.C (record 0 is
0,0,40,200 at file offset 0x23B7F). I checked the call sites and the push order against the bytes; they match the
header. The one must-fix is a wrong sentence in the header: the x1 test comes after the negative-x1 shift, not
before. The 40 and 200 literals should become named screen limits. The placeholder names extra1 and extra2 are
honest and gFar8052 carries its declaration comment. With the header order fixed, the review should pass.
