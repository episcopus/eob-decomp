## Summary

The unit matches (2 of 2, 487 bytes; `make readability UNIT=e07a9`: 0 findings), and the names that rev-sonnet's
review wanted changed (gIconsDDE4, gFn80D2, the icon field, f07A9_010C) are in place and supported by the
bytes I re-checked. The unit is not yet understandable from its source alone in three places. The header and
StartupAlloc say "19EC:0002 allocates three objects" and the globals are named "Obj", but `src/E19EC.C` shows
that the call only reads a segment from the page table. The header calls the three 7500, 6000 and 4096 byte
blocks "the unpack buffers" without evidence, and the unpack work buffers are allocated by `Unpack`
(`src/E1B82.C`). These are must-fix. The shoulds (the SetUnpackBuffer and f1A47_0169 declarations, the
LoadItemData name, the ovl2:0577 comment, the 0x59 / gDF48 alias, the flag comment) are cheap and
cross-file, so they should go in the same commit. Placeholders with no evidence (`Level` is gone; the
`fXXXX_YYYY` callees, `gF43C`, `gFB45`, `g810E`, `g8102`, `g814A`, `gFBD2` and the rest) are fine.
