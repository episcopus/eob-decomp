### Summary

The unit matches (2 of 2, 487 bytes) and the round 2 names are in place, but it is not yet understandable from its
source alone. Three comments say things the bytes contradict: "index 0 is none" (entry 0 is written by a separate
call at ovl2:0029..0033), the order of the StartupAlloc steps (the sound driver loads before the unpack buffer is
recorded), and the QUIRK 07A9:0184 parenthetical about g8102 (the ITEMRMP read is unconditional, ovl2:0478). Two names do
not hold: gFC1A is gSegsFC0C[7], which src/ already names as the page table, and "prints" and "draws" describe callees
that nothing in the repository identifies. The must-fix items need a source change and a re-match, which is the
decompiler's job. The shoulds are declared types (gSoundMap, gA9C0, the `map` parameter), what ds:810E means in
StartupAlloc, the 0x20 flag, the whitepaper citations, the other uses of the flag byte, and the mode-specific size
of the 0xFC00 buffer. The placeholders (fXXXX_YYYY callees, gF43C, gFB45, g811F, gFBD2, gBuf*, g80E2, gFn80D2) are
fine as placeholders; gDF48 has a writer in the overlay that the comment can cite.

## Round 2 summary

review wanted changed (gIconsDDE4, gFn80D2, the icon field, f07A9_010C) are in place and supported by the

LoadItemData name, the ovl2:0577 comment, the 0x59 / gDF48 alias, the flag comment) are cheap and
cross-file, so they should go in the same commit. Placeholders with no evidence (`Level` is gone; the
`fXXXX_YYYY` callees, `gF43C`, `gFB45`, `g810E`, `g8102`, `g814A`, `gFBD2` and the rest) are fine.
