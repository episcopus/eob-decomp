## Summary

The unit is understandable from its source alone. The names that are backed by evidence hold: SetMouseItem (E0D1C.C:300,
E0ACD.C:877), LoadItemData (EOV2.C:449), LoadDecorateIcons (EOV2.C:328), LoadSoundDriver (E177D.C:43), RunMapLoop
(E0ACD.C:842), SetCurrentItem (E0D1C.C:316), gMouseDisabled, gFBCE and gDF48, and the byte-level claims checked here hold
(sizes, order, the flag tests, the QUIRK marks, the dummy at ds:0090, the Entry14 layout). The problems are in the
comments: a constant named for one video mode that the code uses in all of them (SLOT7_BUFFER_SIZE), a sound map
labelled ADLIB when it comes from either driver file, a global called unread that two units read (gSegDF4E), a wrong
ITEMRMP description that treats the map loop as a read, a wrong (0,0) case in the icon call, and four citations with the
wrong line or address. Placeholders stay where no evidence is given (g810E, g80E2, gF43C, gFB45, gSegEFC2, f17A6_197E, f0BD2_05B5, the
externs f19EC_0002 and f1A47_0169), and gFBD2 needs its comment. Nothing is escalated: the matcher and the spec are not in
question. Round 1 findings (the earlier review at e07a9) are in git history (c6326d2, e034148).
